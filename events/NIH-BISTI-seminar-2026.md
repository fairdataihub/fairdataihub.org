## 📝 Event Description

The **NIH Biomedical Information Science and Technology Initiative (BISTI)** seminar series will feature a talk on **"Incentivizing data sharing through Scholar Data, an S-index solution."** BISTI seminars reach roughly 200 NIH staff with interests in data science and data sharing.

Despite growing mandates, data sharing is still not common practice in scientific research. A major reason is the lack of incentives: researchers who share datasets receive little formal credit for their effort. Recognizing this gap, the NIH launched the **Data Sharing Index (S-index) Challenge** in 2024, a $1 million prize competition to develop a standardized metric, inspired by the h-index, that recognizes and rewards researchers for high-quality data sharing.

This seminar will present the outcomes of the first-place winning team led by Bhavesh Patel. It will introduce a vision of the S-index as a metric that quantifies a researcher's data-sharing impact by combining three dataset-level signals: FAIRness, citations, and alternative mentions in software, policy documents, patents, and other non-traditional outputs.

To put this vision into practice, the team built **[Scholar Data](https://scholardata.io)**, an open-source, Google Scholar-like platform where researchers can track their datasets and their data-sharing impact. The seminar will include a live demonstration of Scholar Data, a brief overview of how the underlying metric was validated across millions of real datasets, and a discussion of how funders, repositories, and researchers can use Scholar Data to make data sharing a recognized and integral part of the research process.
